Reference

Glossary.

Plain-English definitions of the terms and acronyms used across the DTx ecosystem.

Group one

Procurement and commercial.

PA23
Procurement Act 2023. The procurement regime chosen for the enduring HomeTest framework.
PSR
Provider Selection Regime. The alternative regime; used as the legal basis for the interim SBS route, but not for the enduring framework.
Open Framework
The NHSE-administered market form chosen under PA23 at the 22 June 2026 SLT playback.
Dynamic Market
The PA23 alternative market form, considered and rejected.
Open Framework
The enduring commercial vehicle: an NHSE-enabled Open Framework under PA23, one end-to-end lot with capability gates on a published roadmap, targeted go-live calendar Q1 2028 in principle at a framework value of circa £250m. Called “Plan A” in the Phase 1 recommendation papers; that shorthand has been retired on this site.
Interim route
How commissioners buy while the Open Framework is built: commissioner-led, through existing frameworks and MOUs (the NHS Online iron deficiency anaemia pathway, now being wound down; SBS10255 for HIV; LPP public health services framework). Guidance to commissioners, not a new vehicle; runs to around January 2028. Called “Plan C” in the Phase 1 papers.
Digital Primary Care Framework option
A fallback considered in Phase 1 and set aside because it could not bridge the near-term gap. Called “Plan B” in the Phase 1 papers.
SBS10255
The NHS SBS Sexual Health Framework, the interim route for the HIV use case.
ACE
The framework used for the current Beta procurement arrangement; not the enduring route.
MOU
Memorandum of Understanding. Used only in HomeTest Alpha and Beta, not as a BAU contracting instrument.
PIN and ITT
Planned Procurement Notice and Invitation to Tender.
Group two

Assurance and regulation.

DTAC
Digital Technology Assessment Criteria.
DCB0129
The clinical safety standard for manufacturers of health IT.
DCB0160
The clinical safety standard for health organisations deploying health IT.
NICE EVA
NICE Early Value Assessment.
Innovator Passport
A reusable assurance route that lets one assurance package be recognised across organisations.
SaMD and non-SaMD
Software as a Medical Device, and the MHRA determination that a configuration is not a medical device.
Group three

Governance and approvals.

IAO
Information Asset Owner.
OBC and FBC
Outline Business Case and Full Business Case.
Approval gates (GAS/GAtS, CEG, DHSC CAB, NHSE Atamis)
The NHS and DHSC governance checkpoints a business case passes through.
Group four

Programme and products.

Six Cornerstones
The six questions that drive the sourcing strategy: ITT specification, volume and value, commercial models, interim route to market, lotting strategy, go-live date.
Self Health Services
The portfolio brand adopted for the DTx programmes in May 2026.
HomeTest, HealthStore, HealthSync
The three DTx products in scope.
ICB
Integrated Care Board.
