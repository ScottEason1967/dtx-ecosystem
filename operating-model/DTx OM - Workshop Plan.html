Workshop programme

This is the workshop programme that settles the six cornerstones the sourcing strategy depends on. It runs from the foundation session on 13 August through to go-live, one workshop per decision, each feeding the plan. The subject matter and the outputs sit on the programme side, and the sessions are where the programme takes ownership of the answers. This is one way to settle the cornerstones in the time available, not the only way; if the programme would rather run it differently, that is theirs to decide. The dates are not arbitrary, because those are the dates the sourcing strategy, and the framework behind it, run to.
Structured working sessions distributed across the five procurement stages, each anchored to a specific decision or output that needs to land in that stage. Ownership of each session and its outputs sits on the programme side; the commercial team attends and consumes the outputs into the sourcing strategy. Every workshop is a click-through with a full agenda: attendees, prep required, objectives, decisions to land and outputs.

How each session is staffed

Every workshop is Programme-led. The lead is a programme team member, still to be identified, which is what the sessions between now and 13 August confirm. Three groups sit in each session, and none of them lead it. The Future State team support and facilitate. Session experts are pulled in for the specific subject. The wider contributors from the core group and the SLT attend where the session needs them, which is for the SLT to confirm for the 2-hour sessions.
