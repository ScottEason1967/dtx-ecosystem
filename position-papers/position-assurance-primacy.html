Position paper · HomeTest Commercial

Assurance is primary, commercial is secondary

NHSE's value-add to the DTx ecosystem is the assurance layer. The commercial machinery is built on top of it. Investment sequence reflects this.

State update (8 June 2026). MHRA confirmed the HomeTest platform is not a Software as a Medical Device. This clarifies the assurance perimeter on the regulatory side: platform-layer assurance does not require MHRA SaMD treatment; supplier kits and lab side remain in scope of medical device regulation. Position

NHSE's value-add to the DTx commercial ecosystem is in the assurance layer. The four components are independent assurance, the NICE EVA evidence interface, assurance portability, and the supplier IG envelope. The commercial machinery (approved list governance, standard call-off, matchmaker mechanics) is built on top of the assurance layer and only works because the assurance layer is credible. Investment sequence reflects this: assurance gets resourced first, commercial follows.

Rationale

Without a credible assurance layer the matchmaker-with-assurance model has nothing to assure. Commissioners and patients do not buy "introductions"; they buy NHSE's underwriting that the suppliers they are being introduced to meet a published standard. Remove the assurance and the commercial machinery becomes a directory service that any other party could provide.

The commercial layer is in turn the operating mechanic that lets assurance scale. A published standard with no admission, removal and contracting machinery would be advisory; the commercial layer is what gives the standard teeth and what makes the introductions happen. Both layers are necessary; the primacy is about sequencing, not value.

Investment sequence reflects this. Programme leadership attention, FTE allocation and discovery effort prioritise the assurance layer first. Commercial mechanics are built into a working assurance shape, not built ahead of it.

The four assurance components

  • Independent assurance. Third-party assurance discipline separate from internal Clinical Safety. Covers regulatory standards, clinical and financial efficacy, independent review of admitted products. Role is defined in the DTx OM Governance lane; the specific external body is not yet named.
  • NICE EVA interface. NICE conditional engagement and evaluation route, already used to select HealthStore COPD Alpha suppliers. Provides the evidence-based admission gate that sits in front of the approved list.
  • Assurance portability. "Assure once, deploy many." A supplier admitted via the published standard carries that assurance across ICBs and configurations, coupled tight to the NICE EVA conditional evaluation interface. Removes the per-ICB re-assurance burden that would otherwise kill scale.
  • Supplier IG envelope. The IG side of the standard the suppliers apply against. Templates, criteria, controllership rules, sub-processor permissions. Boundary with NHSE IG/DPO authority is the load-bearing internal split (see Position · Assurance envelope boundary).

Design principles the assurance primacy enacts

Four design principles sit underneath the assurance-first sequence. The architecture takes the shape it does because these principles are what give the model legitimacy with commissioners, patients and the procurement professionals who buy from it.

  • Commissioning for outcomes, not outputs. Suppliers compete against a published outcome standard rather than against a pre-specified output specification. The standard sets what good looks like (regulatory compliance, clinical efficacy, IG controls, performance thresholds); how the supplier delivers against it is theirs to shape. This is the lens procurement professionals across the system are being asked to adopt, and the assurance layer is what makes it possible at portfolio scale.
  • Standards-enforced market entry. The published standard is the admission gate. Suppliers that do not meet it cannot sell into the ecosystem. The gate sits upstream of the marketplace, not downstream of an award decision, which is what prevents non-compliant suppliers competing on price into commissioners who have no shared baseline to assess them against.
  • Assure once, deploy many. Once a supplier has cleared the published standard they carry that assurance across ICBs and configurations. The portability discipline removes the per-ICB re-assurance burden that would otherwise multiply the cost and timing of scale by the number of commissioners. The reduction in re-work is the operational manifestation of the assurance-primary disposition.
  • Independence of the gate. Third-party independent assurance separates the admission gate from the commercial pressure to admit. The party deciding what good looks like is not the party with revenue exposure to who sells. That separation is what makes the published standard credible to commissioners, patients and the regulatory community.

Reopen triggers

  • A regulatory or NICE position change shifts the assurance layer's centre of gravity (for example, NICE EVA becoming a hard gate or being replaced).
  • Programme-level decision to refocus on commercial revenue generation before the assurance layer matures.
  • Assurance capability fully matures and commercial becomes the binding constraint on scale.
  • A material patient-harm event reframes the relative weight of the two layers.

Dependencies

  • Independent Assurance Lead in post or named. Role exists in the DTx OM Governance lane; needs to be filled or designated.
  • NICE EVA interface operational. Already used at HealthStore Alpha; needs to extend across HomeTest as products mature.
  • Published assurance standard. The substantive criteria suppliers apply against. Consolidated from existing HIV Alpha and Beta IG envelope, clinical safety baseline and lab assurance material.
  • Programme leadership investment-sequence decision. Budget and FTE allocation reflecting assurance primacy. This is the operational consequence of the position.

What this does and does not mean

Does mean: when scarce resource has to be allocated between assurance build and commercial build, assurance wins. The Independent Assurance Lead and the NICE EVA interface come before the standard call-off template polish or the approved-list governance refinement. Does not mean: commercial is unimportant or under-resourced absolutely. It means commercial follows assurance in the sequence, and the commercial build is shaped by what the assurance layer needs to operate.
