Existing routes for current proof points

The current proof-point routes are interim arrangements stood up to support proving. They are not the commercial model. They transition to the matchmaker-with-assurance shape as the model matures.

StatusActive
OwnerHomeTest Commercial lead
Date taken28 May 2026
Review cadenceAs each proof-point exits Beta

Position

The current proof-point routes were stood up to support proving the model at small scale, not to carry portfolio scale. These are the interim instruments. HIV Beta runs on a multi-vehicle structure: the NHS SBS Sexual Health Framework (SBS10255) as the framework call-off for HIV, with direct award where the regulations allow, NHSE-to-LA Memoranda of Understanding, and locally-held sexual-health contracts with the test-delivery suppliers. The MOUs are Alpha and Beta only, not the BAU instrument. The iron deficiency anaemia proof point runs on the NHS Online commission, which is live. The LPP (London Procurement Partnership) public health services framework is a further interim route (lot 1 sexual health / HIV at home; lot 5 NHS health checks and cancer screening at home). ACE is the current Beta arrangement only, not the enduring route. HealthStore COPD Alpha runs on the same existing framework as the procurement route with an NHS Standard Contract carrying SaaS and data schedules. These routes are interim. They are not the commercial model. They transition to the matchmaker-with-assurance shape via Play 7 (Beta-to-BAU transition) as each proof-point matures.

The current routes: primary-source detail

HIV Beta, three vehicles. NHSE-to-LA Memoranda of Understanding with two local authorities (no payment between NHSE and the LAs). NHSE-to-supplier via the NHS SBS Sexual Health Framework (SBS10255), with direct award where the regulations allow. ACE is the current Beta arrangement for this supply only, not the enduring route. NHSE is counterparty for supplier integration and platform development only, not for the tests themselves. LA-to-supplier for test delivery via the local authorities' existing sexual-health contracts. NHSE is not the commercial counterparty for the tests themselves. Public Beta commercial route is not yet defined and is an open item.

LPP public health services framework, further interim route. The London Procurement Partnership public health services framework is available as an additional commissioner-led interim instrument alongside the NHS Online iron deficiency anaemia commission and the NHS SBS Sexual Health Framework (SBS10255). Lot 1 covers sexual health / HIV at home; lot 5 covers NHS health checks and cancer screening at home. Interim only, without prejudice to the enduring the Open Framework position.

HealthStore COPD Alpha, existing framework plus NHS Standard Contract. An existing NHSE framework used as the Alpha procurement route. Contract pattern is the NHS Standard Contract with SaaS and data schedules. Commissioning model is ICB-primary with clinician-prescribed initiation and an episode-payment commercial structure. Steady-state route post-Alpha is open: extend the existing framework, build a HealthStore-specific instrument, or leverage another framework.

Rationale

Both proof-points were stood up under time pressure to demonstrate the operating model with a real cohort. The chosen existing framework was an NHSE route that admits innovation-stage suppliers at speed. None of these instruments were designed to carry portfolio scale or to define the BAU commercial architecture.

Holding the proof-point routes up as the commercial model would be a category error. They were chosen because they were available and fast, not because they reflect the matchmaker-with-assurance position. Treating them as the commercial model would lock the programme into the wrong vehicle and the wrong contracting shape.

The transition path is via Play 7 (Beta-to-BAU transition). As each proof-point matures, the existing arrangements novate or wind down onto the standard call-off plus approved-list shape. Until that transition happens, the existing routes carry on operating without prejudice to the position.

Reopen triggers

  • A proof-point route gets extended materially beyond Beta scale. At that point it stops being an interim and starts shaping the model by default.
  • A new proof-point is stood up using a different interim route not listed here. The position needs to absorb the new route or push back on its use.
  • Play 7 transition mechanics expose a gap that the existing-route position needs to address.
  • NHSE Legal concludes that one of the existing routes must be carried into BAU rather than transitioned off.

Dependencies

  • Play 7 (Beta-to-BAU transition) operational. The transition mechanism that moves proof-points off the interim routes and onto the standard call-off plus approved-list shape.
  • Standard call-off template authored. Receiving instrument for the transition. Without it the existing routes have nowhere to transition to.
  • Approved supplier list register stood up. Receiving qualification mechanism for suppliers currently admitted via the existing route.
  • Public Beta commercial route defined (HIV). Open item; needed before HIV moves from Private Beta into Public Beta scale.
  • Public Beta Direction request (HIV). Per PTT (20 May 2026), no Direction is required for Private Beta because the data movement is system delivery (not NHSE-side analysis). Public Beta likely needs a Direction when NHSE begins to analyse data for its own purposes (cohort MI etc.). Lead time ~6 months. Direction request should land before January 2027 or after March 2027 to avoid the DHSC/NHSE legal-merger transition window.

What this position protects against

Two failure modes. First: the proof-point routes get treated as the commercial model by default because they are what exists, locking the programme into the existing-framework shape forever. Second: the proof-point routes get torn up prematurely before Play 7 transition mechanics are ready, leaving live cohorts without a contracting vehicle. The position holds the line in the middle: current routes carry on operating, transition happens through Play 7, and the matchmaker-with-assurance shape is the destination.
