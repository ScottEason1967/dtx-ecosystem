MoU scope: Alpha and Beta only

Memoranda of Understanding are not the BAU contracting instrument. They served the Alpha and Beta phases because no formal vehicle existed; that role ends with Beta-to-BAU transition.

StatusActive
OwnerHomeTest Commercial lead
Date taken28 May 2026
Review cadenceOn regulatory change only

Position

Memoranda of Understanding are used only in HomeTest Alpha and Beta phases. MoUs are not the BAU contracting instrument and are not used for wave entrants or post-Beta commissioners. Wave entrants sign the standard NHS call-off, with the operational annex pre-filled from the proven pathway template. National platform engagements use whatever instrument the counterparty platform operates (see Position · NHSE role).

Rationale

MoUs were the appropriate instrument during HIV Alpha and Beta because no formal contracting vehicle existed yet. Alpha was a single-partner ad hoc engagement; Beta brought multiple pioneers in under bespoke terms while the model was being shaped. MoUs absorbed that situational ambiguity.

Continuing to use MoUs into BAU would create three failure modes. Variance from bespoke instruments would proliferate (twenty MoUs across twenty commissioners, each subtly different, each requiring bespoke legal review). The standard call-off template, the load-bearing artefact of the interim shape, would be undermined because the alternative would always be a custom MoU. And the audit trail would weaken because MoUs typically lack the discipline of NHS Standard Contract schedules.

Operating BAU through standard call-offs aligns with NHS Standard Contract machinery, gives suppliers and commissioners a familiar vehicle, and lets the operational annex (service spec, KPIs, IG, locations, pricing, reporting) carry the variation rather than the contract structure itself.

Reopen triggers

  • A regulatory change requires MoU as the only acceptable instrument for a specific scenario.
  • A new engagement type emerges that the standard call-off template demonstrably cannot cover.
  • A national platform counterparty (see Play 9) operates only through MoU and HomeTest cannot use the standard call-off in that bilateral.

Dependencies

  • Standard call-off template authored, signed off, published. Without the template, MoUs become the default by absence of alternative.
  • Beta-to-BAU transition mechanism operational. See Play 7. Existing Beta-era MoUs need to novate cleanly onto the standard call-off as the wave model engages.
  • Communicating the position to commissioners and suppliers who joined under Beta MoUs. They need to know the instrument is changing and what that means for them.

What this means in practice

Beta partners (current pioneer commissioners under MoU) continue under their existing MoU until they transition to BAU. Wave entrants (commissioners coming on after the standard call-off template is live) sign the call-off, not an MoU. Existing MoUs are novated onto the standard call-off through Play 7 as their natural review cycle hits.
